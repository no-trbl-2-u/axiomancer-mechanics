**Conclusion: the values that would actually fix this are not in the tunable
registry.** The resolution *thresholds* and effect *ceilings* (max
intensity/duration) don't move the timeout cells because the bottleneck is the
**per-effect base potency/duration defined in the effect JSON libraries**, plus
the **absence of a decisive control/erosion/execute effect** in the mid-game
kits. Raising the ceiling does nothing if no effect's authored values approach it.

Two ways forward (both human-judgement, outside the auto-apply loop):

1. **Add per-effect potency/duration to the tunable registry** (e.g. base DoT
   damage and base duration of the workhorse damage/control effects the mixed and
   strategist kits actually use). Then a future focused tick can A/B them directly
   and the loop can resolve decisions 1 & 2 with evidence.
2. **Hand-tune the effect content** in the JSON libraries — strengthen the
   damage/control effects (and/or add a decisive control/erosion effect) for the
   mid-game stall enemies (`iron-covenanter`, `apostate-abbot`, `the-tithewarden`,
   `contrarian-revenant` — all ~100% timeout while the player dominates the damage
   race), then re-run this focused tick to confirm fights resolve before the cap.

The genuinely *losing*-the-damage-race cells (`l50-mixed-hard`/the-schismarch,
`l50-mixed-normal`/graveward-keeper, playerToEnemy < 1) are content/matchup
problems threshold tuning can't touch — inspect the mixed kit's effects vs those
enemies' resists/cleanse. The L50 boss defeat cells
(`warrant-of-the-void`, `the-last-consensus`) remain owner-deferred.
