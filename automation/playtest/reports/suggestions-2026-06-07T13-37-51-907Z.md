## Propose-only (needs human judgement)

The dominant failure mode is **structural, not a global-constant miss.** Two clusters drive nearly all of the off-band aggregate, and neither is reachable by a registry tunable:

- **Timeouts, not defeats, are the real failure at mid/high level.** Most off-band cells are high-engagement fights the player simply cannot *close out* within the round cap, not fights they lose. `l50-mixed-normal` vs `graveward-keeper`: 100% timeout, 80.0/80 avg rounds, 54 skill uses/run, dmg ratio 815 — the player is landing everything and still can't finish. Same shape at `l15-strategist-normal` vs `reef-barnacle-colony` (89% timeout, 47 skill uses) and `l15-defensive-easy` vs `apprentice-heretic` (84% timeout). These cells score as "failures" (0–16% resolution) despite *excellent* status-effect engagement. **Proposal:** treat a timeout against a heavily-debuffed defensive enemy as a soft win, OR add a "the enemy yields when sufficiently worn down" resolution path, OR expose the round cap / a damage-floor-vs-defensive-stance term as a tunable. Without one of these the band term can never close for the elite/defensive roster.

- **Level-1 hard/normal is over-tuned per-enemy, not globally.** `reef-barnacle-colony` (17% resolution, 55% defeat across 4 cells) and `tolltaker-of-the-ford` (L1 mixed-hard 91% defeat) blow up the L1 row, while `petty-cutpurse`/`thicket-ambusher`/`rimeclaw-prowler` at the same tier resolve ~95–100%. A global `enemy.statPerLevel` cut would trivialize the already-fine L1 enemies to fix two outliers. **Proposal:** rebalance the `reef-barnacle-colony` and `tolltaker-of-the-ford` stat blocks / effect kits directly (per-enemy), or add a per-enemy difficulty floor. Evidence: `l1-defensive-hard` 94% defeat in 5.7 rounds with only 1.5 skill uses/run — the player dies before any status play can develop, which is also the worst engagement cell in the matrix.

- **L50 is trivially won across all difficulties — player power outscales late enemies.** Easy/normal/hard all resolve ~100% by L50 (`l50-*-hard` all 1.0). This needs a per-level or super-linear enemy scaling term; `enemy.statPerLevel` is a single global slope and cannot bend the curve without breaking L1. **Proposal:** a difficulty-scaled or late-game enemy power curve (new tunable / formula).

- **The strategist — the doctrine's witness — underperforms at low/mid level.** Strategist has the most skill uses (20.7/run) yet sits at 44% objective engagement and owns the worst off-band cells (`l15-strategist-normal` 3% resolution / 89% timeout). The status play is happening; it just can't convert to resolution against defensive elites (same timeout root cause as #1). Worth inspecting strategist skill-cost vs token-generation timing — possibly a skill-cost tunable not yet in the registry.
